BAKED BY NETTA WEBSITE — UPDATED WITH REVIEWS

The website design and existing content have been kept in place. A matching Reviews section has been added, including a customer review form with star ratings and an optional photo upload.

PUBLISHING ON NETLIFY
1. Go to your Netlify site dashboard.
2. Deploy the updated ZIP using the site's deploy area / drag-and-drop deploy.
3. After the new deploy is live, open your site and submit a test review.
4. In Netlify, open Forms to view submitted reviews and any uploaded photos.

IMPORTANT
Netlify Forms collects review submissions and photo uploads. Submissions are not automatically published as public reviews. Review them first, then add approved customer testimonials/photos to the review display so you can prevent spam and get permission before publishing customer photos.

Original ordering and social links remain unchanged.
